# MediLab Nexus Doctor Guide

## Main Goal

Doctors and reporting specialists use MediLab Nexus to interpret sonography studies, complete reports, and control final report release.

## Main Pages

- Dashboard
- Patients
- Patient Records
- Sonography Worklist
- Scan Reports

> Screenshot placeholder: doctor reporting workspace.

## Start-of-Shift Routine

1. Sign in.
2. Open Sonography Worklist.
3. Review what is ready for interpretation.
4. Check for critical flags.

## Reporting Workflow

1. Confirm the correct patient and order.
2. Review the history and study context.
3. Open Scan Reports.
4. Complete the title, findings, and impression.
5. Use the right ultrasound template where needed.
6. Preview the report.
7. Release the report only when clinically complete.

## Clinical Discipline

- Use precise wording in findings and impression.
- Ensure the final report belongs to the correct patient.
- Flag critical findings promptly.
- Avoid releasing partially reviewed reports.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Using the wrong order or patient.
- Leaving key findings only in draft notes.
- Releasing before final review.
- Forgetting to preview the final printable report.

## Escalate When

- The wrong study appears ready for reporting.
- The scan documentation is incomplete.
- The report preview does not match the intended final output.
- A clinically urgent case needs immediate communication.
