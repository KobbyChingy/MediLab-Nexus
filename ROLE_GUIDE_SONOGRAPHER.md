# MediLab Nexus Sonographer Guide

## Main Goal

Sonographers use MediLab Nexus to manage the imaging queue, update appointment status, and prepare accurate handoff information for reporting.

## Main Pages

- Dashboard
- Patients
- Patient Records
- Sonography Worklist
- Scan Reports

> Screenshot placeholder: sonography worklist.

## Start-of-Shift Routine

1. Sign in.
2. Open Sonography Worklist.
3. Review scheduled, arrived, and scanning patients.
4. Identify any critical or priority cases early.

## During Patient Flow

1. Confirm the patient by name and Trace Code.
2. Update the appointment status as the patient arrives.
3. Mark the study as Scanning when acquisition begins.
4. Enter the sonographer name and relevant study details.
5. Mark the study ready for handoff when the scan is complete.

## Draft Reporting Support

Use Scan Reports when your workflow requires structured draft preparation before doctor review.

Focus on:

- Correct patient and order selection
- Clear document context for the reporting clinician
- Accurate structured findings support inside the single report document
- Timely critical flag escalation

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Starting a scan under the wrong patient.
- Leaving appointment statuses outdated.
- Failing to mark critical studies.
- Handing off incomplete information to the doctor.

## Escalate When

- The wrong service or study appears in the queue.
- The patient details do not match the request.
- A critical finding needs clinician attention.
- The reporting user cannot see the completed study.
