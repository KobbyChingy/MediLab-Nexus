# MediLab Nexus Manager Guide

## Main Goal

Managers use MediLab Nexus to keep daily operations, staff activity, reporting flow, and financial performance aligned.

## Main Pages

- Dashboard
- Patients
- Patient Records
- Sonography Worklist
- Scan Reports
- Operations Report
- Expenses
- Services
- User Management
- Alerts
- Settings

## Start-of-Day Review

1. Open Dashboard.
2. Check queue pressure and urgent flags.
3. Review Sonography Worklist for bottlenecks.
4. Open Operations Report to review collections, expenses, and staff activity.

## Daily Management Tasks

- Confirm that front-desk intake is flowing correctly.
- Review pending scan reports and delayed handoff.
- Monitor expense trends.
- Review outstanding balances.
- Confirm services and pricing remain current.
- Check Alerts for internal coordination needs.

## Supervisory Tasks

- Review user status and lockouts.
- Confirm reports are being finalized on time.
- Check that financial entries are complete.
- Escalate repeated workflow or staffing issues.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Looking only at revenue without checking expenses and outstanding balances.
- Ignoring queue bottlenecks until delays affect patients.
- Leaving inactive or incorrect user accounts unchanged.
- Overlooking internal alerts and unresolved critical flags.

## Escalate When

- Scan or reporting turnaround becomes abnormal.
- Revenue entries and workflow activity do not align.
- Multiple users report access problems.
- A serious operational or compliance concern appears in Audit Logs.
