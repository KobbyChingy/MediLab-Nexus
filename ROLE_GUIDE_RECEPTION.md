# MediLab Nexus Reception Guide

## Main Goal

Reception users keep patient intake, service selection, front-desk payments, and report handoff organized and traceable.

## Main Pages

- Dashboard
- Patients
- Patient Records
- Expenses
- Services
- Scan Reports
- Settings

## Start-of-Shift Routine

1. Sign in.
2. Open Dashboard.
3. Check recent patients and open work.
4. Confirm the printer and browser pop-up settings are working for previews.

## Registering a Patient

1. Open Patients.
2. Search first to avoid duplicate registration.
3. Enter the patient's name, phone number, gender, and other required details.
4. Confirm or note the Trace Code.
5. Save the registration.

## Starting Intake

1. From Patients, attach the required service.
2. Capture payer details carefully.
3. If payment is collected immediately, record it correctly.
4. Direct the patient to the next department.

## Report Pickup and Printing

1. Open Scan Reports only after the reporting clinician finishes the report.
2. Preview the report.
3. Print only the final approved or released document according to facility policy.

## Front-Desk Expenses

Use Expenses to record any cash outflow or refund that must remain visible in the operational record.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Registering the same patient twice.
- Handing out a report before the clinician finishes it.
- Recording payment against the wrong patient.
- Ignoring the Trace Code during follow-up.

## Escalate When

- A patient record cannot be found.
- A clinician says the wrong service was attached.
- A report appears unfinished.
- The account is locked or the system does not load.
